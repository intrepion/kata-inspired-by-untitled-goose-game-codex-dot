Field notes

Walk up to something and use your beak. Carry objects to the straw nest in the near corner and drop them there. The caretaker can take things back if she catches you, so use a honk to distract her.

The ribbon is there for you to play with too. Tap the irrigation valve and watch what happens.

On touch screens, hold a direction or Run; tap the other actions. The game pauses when the page is hidden.
